BOOK2AUDIO: turn an e-book into an audiobook
=============================================

Book2Audio opens a page in your web browser. You drop in an .epub or .pdf book, pick a voice, and it makes
an audiobook (.m4b file) that you can play in any audiobook app. It runs on your own computer. Nothing is
installed: this whole folder is the program.


1. FIRST TIME ONLY: unzip it properly
-------------------------------------
- Right-click the zip file, choose Properties. If you see a box "Unblock" at the bottom, tick it and press OK.
- Right-click the zip file again, choose "Extract All", and extract it to a normal folder such as Documents
  or Desktop. (Do not run it from inside the zip, and do not put it in "Program Files".)


2. Start it
-----------
- Open the folder and double-click  "Start Book2Audio.bat".
- A black window opens and your browser shows the Book2Audio page. KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you work.
- If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", press "More info", then "Run anyway". It says this because the
  file is new to Windows, not because anything is wrong. Only do this if you trust where you got the zip.


3. Make your first audiobook
----------------------------
- Open "Get ready" at the top of the page. It tells you what is missing. For the FREE voice, press
  "Install it for me" (needs internet, about 100 MB, once), then download one voice from the voice list.
- Drop your book on the page, tick the chapters you want, choose a voice (press "Sample" to hear it on your
  own book), and press "Convert to audiobook".
- When it says your audiobook is ready, press "Show in folder". It is saved in your Music folder, in a folder
  called book2audio.


If something does not work
--------------------------
- The black window closes at once: the folder is probably still inside the zip. Extract it first (step 1).
- The free voice says a file such as VCRUNTIME or MSVCP140 is missing: install Microsoft's free "Visual C++
  Redistributable" (search for it on microsoft.com), then try again.
- Anything else: the black window shows what the program is doing. Keep it open and send its text to whoever
  gave you this folder.


Paid voices (ElevenLabs, Google) and the Claude clean-up
--------------------------------------------------------
These send the text of your book over the internet to that company, and the company charges you. The page
always says so before anything is spent. Their keys (like a password for your account) are pasted into the
page, kept only while the program runs, and never saved to a file.


Stopping
--------
Press "Close Book2Audio" on the page, or close the black window. Anything already made is kept: next time,
drop the same book again and press Convert to carry on where it stopped.


Removing it
-----------
Delete this folder. Your settings, voices and unfinished work are in  %LOCALAPPDATA%\book2audio  (paste that
into the address bar of a File Explorer window); delete that folder too if you want everything gone. Finished
audiobooks in Music\book2audio are yours and stay.
